Back Story: After the events of TimeSplitters
Future Perfect, the future was, well, perfect. Do we truly know if
it was perfect? This is a story about what could 
have happened if the future was not so perfect.

Story: During the final battle, with Crow, there
were two Cortezes; only one made it back home.
The 2nd Cortez became a paradox. The timeline is
collapsing, and once again, the turn of the TimeSplitters
are at hand. Crow is taken out, so why the return
of humanity's greatest foe? Is the future truly
set in stone?
